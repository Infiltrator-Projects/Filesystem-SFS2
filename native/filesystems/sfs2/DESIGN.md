# SFS2 engineering design and provenance

The authoritative on-disk format, algorithms, feature gates, recovery semantics and verification requirements are integrated into the [unified technical reference](../../../docs/SFS2-TECHNICAL-REFERENCE.md) and [installed PDF](../../../docs/SFS2.pdf). This supersedes the duplicated format chapters in the earlier design document.

The [former design and source-layout record](https://github.com/Infiltrator-Projects/Filesystem-SFS2/blob/f7a438f76ebaadebb5ab2b6ba4f867b1fb0c3bec/native/filesystems/sfs2/DESIGN.md) is preserved at its exact historical revision for attribution, earlier adapter layouts and migration evidence. Earlier Linux/Windows adapter status is not a production capability claim. Current provider status is defined by the [README](../../../README.md), exported Common V2 operation masks and exact-revision qualification. The engine, not the host or consumer, owns filesystem semantics. Reject malformed media and unqualified features; do not infer write, recovery or firmware support from format knowledge or a successful compile.
